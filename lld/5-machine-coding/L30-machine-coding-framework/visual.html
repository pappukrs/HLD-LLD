Check yourself

Why write the driver before the logic?
It fixes the public API, gives you something to run after every feature, and is the demo at the end.
Minute 45, only one of four must-haves runs. What now?
Say it out loud and cut scope: finish the simplest version of each remaining must-have, drop good-to-haves, keep the demo slot.
What do you inject in almost every problem?
Clock, id generator, repository and the varying strategy. Sometimes a notifier or gateway.
What does the interviewer check first in the demo?
That every must-have runs end to end, then how a new requirement would plug in without editing the core.
